**Severities (fixed, as in 5.8 and 6.10a).** High = a caller can make root do something outside the allow-list, or a golden rule (2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 10) can break with no test failing.
Medium = a defence exists but its proof is missing or cannot fail, or a gap that needs one more mistake to become High. Low/Info = hygiene and docs.

## 1. Verdict in plain words

- **No High.** `-y` exists only with the private `--dbpath` and `--logfile /dev/null`; both flows that sync go through `update_job`; the real lists change only through
  `move_lists_in`, after the private dry run equals the confirmed list; no `db.lck` is ever created, changed or removed; nothing is deleted; the one rename is inside the real
  `sync/` by construction; `TIDEWISE_LISTS` cannot be influenced by the caller.
- **Two Medium findings, both confirmed by me, both open for the human's decision (they change behaviour):** **R-01** two helpers at once are not serialised during the private
  part, so one can copy a list the other just replaced; **R-02** a refused update cannot converge, because the app's list comes from real lists that a refusal now leaves stale.
  R-02 also shows that a sentence of decision 0007 ("costs one refused update and no damage") was wrong; it is corrected.
- **Seven Low or Info findings:** three fixed (R-03, R-05, R-06), one documented (R-04), three documented as residuals (R-07, R-08, R-09). Three of the reviewer's
  "mutations that would survive" got a test or a documented limit.

**Mutations the reviewer thinks would survive** (its list, with my notes):
1. `O_NOFOLLOW` removed from the **source** open in `stage()`, and 2. the `fstat` re-check there: **survive**, because `refreshed_lists` already rejects links with `symlink_metadata`; the
   race they close (a swap between that look and the open) cannot be set up in a single-threaded unit test. Accepted as defence in depth, stated here. (The same mutation on the **staged**
   file does fail a test; I ran it.)
3. `set_permissions(0o644)` removed: **survived**; **fixed** with `a_leftover_with_another_mode_is_set_to_0644`, mutation-checked (removed: it fails; restored: it passes).
4. `sync_all` calls deleted while the trace markers stay: **survives**; an fsync cannot be observed without a crash. The trace test pins the order of the steps, not that the call does
   what it says. Accepted and stated.
5. The owner check on the `local` link: survives (no unit test can create a foreign-owned link without being root). The container test runs as root but plants none. Accepted.
6-13: caught by the tests the reviewer named (truncate, repository-name filter, private-lock look, Moved/Untouched, second dry run, move order, signature rule).
14. Concurrency protection: none exists (R-01).

**Things the reviewer checked and found sound** (all of them also read by me while fixing): `-y` only with the private pair (and `y_only_ever_comes_with_...` is strong); both flows share
`update_job`; no other path refreshes the real lists; every lock look is `symlink_metadata` through `looks_present`; the folder mode check is `& 0o7777 == 0o711`, the umask is set
at start and the mode is looked at again; staged and target paths share a parent by construction; no deletes; the journal field is an enum with two words; `renamed` is counted
correctly on partial failures; the Runner trait and the Fake agree; one `#[expect]` for the rename; `reset_world` leaves no private state between container tests.
