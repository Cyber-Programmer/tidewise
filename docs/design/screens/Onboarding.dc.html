Welcome to Tidewise

It looks after your Linux PC, the way you're used to on other systems: clear out junk, see what's taking space, remove apps, and see what's running. All in plain language.

  • Nothing changes without you seeing it first
  • Works the same on Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch, Mint and more
  • No account and no tracking

Here's your PC

Tidewise adapts to how your system installs and runs things. You don't need to change anything.

Allow admin fixes?

Some fixes, like clearing old package downloads, need admin rights. Tidewise asks through your system's own password prompt and never sees your password.

Your privacy

Tidewise has no account and sends no usage data. It only goes online to check for new versions.
